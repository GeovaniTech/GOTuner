Draft full description:

> GOTuner helps you tune a guitar in standard E–A–D–G–B–E tuning. Play one open string to see its detected frequency, nearest supported guitar target and deviation in cents. Clear guidance tells you whether to tighten or loosen the string. A dark interface and tuning indicator make small adjustments easier to follow. Microphone audio is processed on your device. English and Portuguese interfaces are available. The Android release includes Firebase usage analytics and crash diagnostics; see the privacy policy for details.

Do not claim professional calibration, perfect noise rejection, offline-only data collection, endorsement or store approval.
